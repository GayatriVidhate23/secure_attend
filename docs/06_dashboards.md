# Faculty & Admin Dashboards

SecureAttend AI provides robust management interfaces for both administrators and faculty members to oversee the attendance lifecycle.

## Faculty Dashboard (Mobile & Web)
- **Session Management**: Faculty can select an Academic Division and a Subject to initiate a Live Attendance Session.
- **Dynamic QR Display**: During a live session, the dashboard displays the rotating QR code in full screen for students to scan.
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: The dashboard polls the server to show a live count and list of students who have successfully marked their attendance.
- **Student Roster**: Faculty can view a list of all students assigned to their authorized divisions, checking enrollment status and attendance history.

## Admin Panel (Web)
- **Academic Hierarchy Management**: Admins define the structure of the institution—Semesters, Divisions, and Subjects.
- **User Provisioning**: Admins can create new Faculty and Student accounts, or bulk import them.
- **Face Enrollment Management**: Admins have the authority to enroll student faces or reset corrupted face embeddings.
- **Global Reporting**: Comprehensive statistical views of attendance across the entire institution, identifying trends or students with critically low attendance.
